Research

I’m a Ph.D. student in Computer Science at UTEP. I started out in Materials Engineering at UACJ, moved into data science for my M.S., and now work on the part of machine learning I find most interesting: knowing what a model doesn’t know. I focus on three questions about KANs:

01

How do we train them well? Adaptive optimizer sequencing that runs L-BFGS first, tested with ablations against baselines (MICAI 2026, code on GitHub).

02

Can we read them? Extracting symbolic formulas from trained KANs, so a model can be read as an equation.

03

When are they wrong? Hessian-based estimates of a model’s confidence and expected error.
