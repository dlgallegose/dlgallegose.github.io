Research

My research is in machine learning, with a primary focus on Kolmogorov-Arnold Networks (KANs), a neural network architecture that replaces fixed activation functions on nodes with learnable activation functions on edges. Current work:

  • Optimization for KANs. Adaptive optimizer sequencing (L-BFGS first), validated with ablations against baselines, with open-source code.
  • Interpretability. Turning trained KANs into readable mathematical formulas (Julia), so a model can be inspected rather than just trusted.
  • Confidence and error estimation. Methods to estimate how confident a model is and how wrong it is likely to be, including Hessian-based analysis.
