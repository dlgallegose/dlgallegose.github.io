Research Interests

My research is in machine learning, with a primary focus on Kolmogorov-Arnold Networks (KANs) — a neural network architecture that replaces fixed activation functions on nodes with learnable activation functions on edges. I’m interested in:

  • How KANs compare to traditional multilayer perceptrons in accuracy and interpretability
  • Efficient training and scaling of KAN-based models
  • Applications of KANs to real-world prediction and forecasting problems
